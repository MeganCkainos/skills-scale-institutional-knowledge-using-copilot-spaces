# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation. This folder is the central entry point for how teams plan, deliver, release, and continuously improve work using a consistent, customer-focused process.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first:** prioritize outcomes and usability.
- **Iterative delivery:** ship small, testable increments.
- **Clear ownership:** assign accountable owners for decisions and delivery.
- **Data-informed decisions:** measure impact and adjust based on evidence.
- **Psychological safety:** encourage feedback, learning, and blameless improvement.

## Key Roles

- **Project Manager (PM):** drives delivery coordination, schedules, risks, and communication.
- **Product Manager (PdM):** defines outcomes, prioritizes backlog, and measures value.
- **Developers:** design, implement, test, and review deliverables.
- **QA/Testing:** validates acceptance criteria and release readiness.
- **Stakeholders:** provide direction, input, and approvals.

For detailed role and persona descriptions, see [octoacme-roles-and-personas.md](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md).

## Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a five-phase lifecycle from initiation to retrospective. Work is planned into prioritized, shippable increments and delivered iteratively with clear acceptance criteria and review gates.

Roles and personas clarify ownership across planning and delivery: PMs coordinate execution and risk, PdMs define value and priorities, developers build and validate solutions, QA/Testing confirms readiness, and stakeholders provide business alignment and approvals.

Communication is structured through recurring standups, weekly alignment syncs, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths for blockers and dependencies. Quality is maintained through testing, code review, and release checks, while risk management is handled through ongoing identification, mitigation tracking, and proactive communication.
