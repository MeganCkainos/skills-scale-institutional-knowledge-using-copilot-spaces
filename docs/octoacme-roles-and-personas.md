# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects assess technical feasibility, validate solution designs, and identify technical risks early. They help ensure delivery plans align with architecture constraints and long-term platform goals.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate technical feasibility for proposed initiatives
- Validate solution designs against architecture standards
- Identify and communicate technical risks and trade-offs
- Recommend mitigation plans for architecture and integration risks

### Goals
- Reduce architecture-related rework and delivery delays
- Maintain solution consistency, scalability, and maintainability
- Enable informed technical decision-making across teams

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design walkthroughs
- Technical risk summaries in planning and status meetings
- Decision records and solution proposals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provides design guidance, reviews implementation approaches, and supports technical unblockers.
- **Product Managers:** Aligns feature intent with technical constraints, scope options, and sequencing trade-offs.
- **Project Managers:** Surfaces technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation actions for delivery planning.

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons manage external stakeholder communication, gather feedback, and ensure work remains aligned with business objectives. They bridge product direction with partner and customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate communication with external teams and stakeholders
- Collect and synthesize stakeholder feedback for planning
- Clarify business priorities, constraints, and expectations
- Escalate misalignment risks and propose resolution paths

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder alignment and transparency
- Reduce ambiguity in cross-team expectations
- Strengthen confidence in delivery progress and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and update meetings
- Structured feedback summaries and decision notes
- Cross-team alignment sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Communicates external needs and context that affect implementation priorities or constraints.
- **Product Managers:** Partners on translating feedback into product priorities and acceptance expectations.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinates stakeholder timelines, communications cadence, and escalation channels.

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, testing strategy, and acceptance criteria to ensure releases meet reliability and usability expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards for features and releases
- Establish test strategy across functional and non-functional areas
- Refine acceptance criteria with product and development leads
- Track quality risks, defects, and readiness signals

### Goals
- Improve release confidence and product reliability
- Detect defects early and reduce production incidents
- Ensure consistent quality expectations across teams

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and quality gates
- Defect triage and readiness reviews
- Quality reports and release readiness updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborates on test coverage, defect resolution priorities, and quality improvements.
- **Product Managers:** Aligns acceptance criteria and validates feature behavior against user expectations.
- **Project Managers:** Provides quality status, risk indicators, and go/no-go input for milestones.

---

## Deployment Coordinator

### Role Summary
Deployment Coordinators manage release logistics, environment readiness, and post-deployment support coordination to enable smooth and predictable releases.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release logistics, sequencing, and deployment windows
- Coordinate environment setup and readiness checks
- Manage release runbooks and communication plans
- Coordinate post-deployment monitoring and support handoffs

### Goals
- Minimize deployment disruption and rollback risk
- Improve release predictability and operational readiness
- Ensure fast response to post-release issues

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and deployment checklists
- Launch readiness updates and incident channels
- Post-deployment summaries and handoff notes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Coordinates release packaging, change validation, and deployment support expectations.
- **Product Managers:** Aligns release timing with feature commitments and customer communication needs.
- **Project Managers:** Syncs release milestones, dependencies, and contingency plans within project schedules.

---

## Knowledge Transfer Owner

### Role Summary
Knowledge Transfer Owners capture process improvements, lessons learned, and operational knowledge so teams retain context and continuously improve delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Document key process changes, decisions, and outcomes
- Capture lessons learned from retrospectives and incidents
- Maintain shared knowledge resources and onboarding references
- Drive follow-through on knowledge preservation actions

### Goals
- Preserve institutional knowledge across projects and teams
- Reduce repeated mistakes through documented learning
- Accelerate onboarding and cross-team collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Retrospective outputs and improvement trackers
- Process documentation updates and knowledge-sharing sessions
- Lessons-learned briefs and handoff summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Captures implementation learnings, technical practices, and recurring pain points.
- **Product Managers:** Documents product decision context and outcome insights for future planning.
- **Project Managers:** Records delivery learnings, risk patterns, and process improvement actions.
